# User responsibilities

Velyos Deck is a local DJ workstation: it imports, analyzes, enhances, mixes, and
plays back **audio files that you already have on your computer**.

You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights to every file you use with
Velyos Deck. This includes:

- Files you created or own.
- Files licensed to you (purchases, promo pools, label-provided promos).
- Public-domain or openly licensed material (e.g. Creative Commons), used within
  the terms of that license.

## What Velyos Deck does **not** do

The public Velyos Deck does **not** include any feature for downloading audio
from streaming platforms (Spotify, TIDAL, SoundCloud, YouTube, etc.). Downloading
from those services generally violates their terms of service and may infringe
copyright. That functionality is intentionally **not part of this project**.

## Performing in public

Playing recorded music in public (clubs, events, streams) may require performance
licenses from collecting societies or venues, independently of Velyos Deck. That
is between you, the venue, and the relevant rights organizations.

This document is not legal advice. If you are unsure about your rights, consult a
qualified professional.
